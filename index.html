              Where every number comes from

                News reports are de-duplicated by headline, but one incident can still be covered by several outlets, and many crimes are never reported in the news. Treat these counts as indicators, not official totals.

                How counting works

                • Stories are collected every hour from public English and Tamil news feeds.
                • Headlines are sorted into crime types and districts by keywords.
                • The same incident covered by several outlets is counted once. English and Tamil stories are matched by type, district and day.
                • Mistakes can be reported with the flag on each story and are fixed by hand.
                • Inflation is read from MoSPI's official API; state debt and weekly borrowing from RBI publications.
                • Official yearly crime totals come from NCRB via data.gov.in.
